# Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Professional legal website. Trust, authority, clarity, calm.

## Design Philosophy

Minimal interface, large whitespace, strong typography, editorial layout.

### Reference Inspirations

Apple, Stripe, Notion

### Target Audience

Individuals seeking legal consultation, companies seeking legal services, clients searching for reliable legal information.

### Interface Goals

Fast reading, high trust perception, clear service presentation, strong call to action.

## Do & Don't

- **Do**: Strong headings, maintain whitespace, limited colors, simple layout
- **Don't**: Bright colors, heavy gradients, cluttered layouts

## Agent Prompt Guide

Use navy primary color, gold accent for highlights, minimal layout with strong typography. Generate responsive components using Tailwind CSS. Design sections with large spacing. Use professional imagery for lawyer profile.
