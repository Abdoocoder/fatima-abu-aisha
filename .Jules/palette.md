## 2025-05-22 - [Form Accessibility and Feedback]
**Learning:** Standardizing form feedback and accessibility markers (red asterisks + aria-required) across the app improves trust and clarity. Adding a character counter for textareas provides immediate feedback on input limits, preventing submission errors before they happen.
**Action:** Always include visual and ARIA indicators for required fields, and provide character counters for fields with length constraints. Use MagneticButton for primary actions to add a touch of delight.
